Hi! I'm Tanner. I like logic, functional programming, category theory, and formal verification. I'm currently a Member of Technical Staff at Logical Intelligence. I've worked in Amazon's Automated Reasoning Group, I contribute to open source formalization libraries like [Mathlib](https://lean-lang.org/use-cases/mathlib/) and [CSlib](https://github.com/leanprover/cslib/tree/main), and I am a primary author of [Algolean](https://github.com/Shreyas4991/Algolean/tree/main).

I did my undergraduate degree at Penn in mathematical logic and my masters in computer science. I played two years of D1 football, nowadays I play adult league baseball, lift weights, and sprint. I like to read, primarily analytic philosophy, philosophy of mind and consciousness, eastern philosophy, and mathematics. I am also passionate about veganism and liberation for all beings.
